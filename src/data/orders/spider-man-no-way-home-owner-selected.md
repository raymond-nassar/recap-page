# Spider-Man: No Way Home (Owner selections)

Compiled for this project from the owner's three selections, in the supplied order.
[Selection and edition decision](https://github.com/raymond-nassar/recap-page/issues/686#issuecomment-5971795523).
The standard collections below expand to original individual comics, not collection records or
the extra issues in larger editions. This is separate from the existing Comic Book Herald companion.
The factual expansion and source boundary are recorded in
[DATA_PROVENANCE.md](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md#owner-selected-no-way-home-companion).
