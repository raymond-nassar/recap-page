# MCU Prep: Eternals

The owner selected these three collections for this project. Their verified original issues are
expanded here in selection order, then issue order within each volume. This is not a Comic Book
Herald guide, and collection identifiers are not substituted for original comics.

The first Kirby volume stops at Eternals (1976) #11. The Gaiman and Romita Jr. collection contains
the seven-issue 2006 miniseries. The owner approved Only Death Is Eternal as the third selection's
correct first-volume title; its Eternals (2021) #1-6 boundary does not expand to the full Gillen run.
The original supplied subtitle and all three selection positions remain in the
[maintained provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md#owner-authored-eternals-selection)
and its linked factual edition and identity ledger. The owner-authored scope and subtitle decision
are recorded in [the intake Issue](https://github.com/raymond-nassar/recap-page/issues/684).
Film-tone and adaptation comparisons are not presented as verified facts.
