# Secret Wars Fast Track Trade Reading Order

This project-authored factual checklist follows the ten-section sequence supplied by the owner
from [Comic Book Herald's Secret Wars fast track](https://www.comicbookherald.com/the-complete-marvel-reading-order-guide/secret-wars-reading-order/#secretwars0).
The selection is re-expressed as original issue identities rather than copied guide narrative or
retailer links. Its MCU Prep counterpart uses this same checklist under a different catalog name.
Issue read progress is shared, while each imported Reading List keeps its own name and sections.

Time Runs Out uses the [guide's issue-by-issue interleaving](https://www.comicbookherald.com/the-complete-marvel-reading-order-guide/secret-wars-reading-order/#secretwars6)
within each volume. The Ultimate detour stays between volumes 2 and 3. Avengers #41 belongs to
volume 3, despite the fast-track prose naming volume 2. New Avengers deliberately selects only
#1-3 from its first collection, not the entire book. The final section includes the original
Free Comic Book Day Secret Wars #0 and Secret Wars (2015) #1-9, as the owner requested and the
guide's updated collection contents confirm.

Exact issue identities come from the metadata mirror and were checked on 2026-10-03.
See [the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for the volume evidence,
source discrepancies and the distinction between this factual compilation and a licence grant.
No comic image bytes or source narrative are included.
