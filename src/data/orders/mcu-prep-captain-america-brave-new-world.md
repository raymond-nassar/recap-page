## Hulk Vol. 1: Red Hulk

The 2009 paperback, ISBN 9780785128823, by Jeph Loeb and Ed McGuinness selects Hulk (2008) #1-6 and the Puny Little Man backup from Wolverine (2003) #50.
The corresponding Premiere hardcover is a distinct 2008 edition, ISBN 9780785128816.
Opening Wolverine #50 opens and records progress for the full original issue, including its unrelated lead material; no per-story identity or progress is created.
The first collection does not reveal Red Hulk's identity or contain the later Ross-origin explanation. Neither later issue is added.
