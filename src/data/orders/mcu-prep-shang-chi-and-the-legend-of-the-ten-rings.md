# Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled for this project from the [owner's seven-block Comic Book Herald excerpt](https://github.com/raymond-nassar/recap-page/issues/683#issuecomment-5971742989).
The factual upstream is [Comic Book Herald's Shang-Chi guide](https://www.comicbookherald.com/shang-chi-master-of-kung-fu-reading-order/), under **Latest Additions:**, verified on 2026-10-03.
This is the owner-selected excerpt, not the full guide or the complete section. The intervening Atlantis Attacks (2020) #1-5 recommendation is not selected.
The seven selections comprise six collected editions and the standalone Legend one-shot.

Marvel's Voices: Identity (2021) #1 selects the Shang-Chi story only; completion remains whole-issue.
The linked Greg Pak Sword Master collection selects material from #1-6; the checklist retains the six original issues and whole-issue progress.
Titles and issue links are factual original-comic metadata. No external commentary, artwork, branding or direct film-inspiration claim is copied.
