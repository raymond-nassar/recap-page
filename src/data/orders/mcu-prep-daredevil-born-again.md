# MCU Prep: Daredevil: Born Again

An owner-selected companion covering six collection groups and 37 original comics.

The owner supplied five selections and explicitly approved including both Bendis's Out and
Brubaker's first volume within the third selection. This checklist expands the verified
collection boundaries in that order, not a complete external Daredevil guide. Born Again is the
seven-issue arc, and both Zdarsky volumes belong to the 2019 series. See
[DATA_PROVENANCE.md](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md#owner-authored-mcu-prep-daredevil-born-again)
for the bibliographic sources, ordered owner-selection ledger and centrally approved overlaps.
The [owner's intake issue](https://github.com/raymond-nassar/recap-page/issues/681) records the
scope decisions. Selection credit stays with raymond-nassar; retained guide mappings supply
only exact original-issue identities. No collection IDs, provider gaps or edition extras are
silently substituted.
