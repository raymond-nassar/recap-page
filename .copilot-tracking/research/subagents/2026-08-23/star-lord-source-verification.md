## Contrarian Findings

and the same trailing non-sequence link. The page's modified date also predates the snapshot. There is
no evidence of a boundary change.

`Star-Lord: Annihilation - Conquest` is the only structural ambiguity because it is prominently
linked and discussed. It still has no explicit issue-numbered `Collects:` line. Including its four
issues would produce 103 rather than the accepted 99 and would apply a different inclusion rule from
the Rocket and Groot packets. The lane therefore records the ambiguity but finds no evidence for
changing the accepted boundary.

`Captain Marvel (2017) #125-129` is topically unusual but is explicitly included by a `Collects:`
line. Omitting those five issues would silently shorten the source sequence.
