# User Journey: First Reading List

## Persona
- **Who**: New user with an empty library
- **Goal**: Start reading Marvel comics in a useful order
- **Context**: Evening at a desktop, with Marvel Unlimited available
- **Success metric**: Has a Reading List in the library and knows the next issue

## Stage 1: Arrival
**Doing**: Launches the app and lands on Home.

**Thinking**: "How do I want to get into this?"

**Target experience**: One question and three large choices establish the available reading modes
without a wall of individual cards.

## Stage 2: Orientation
**Doing**: Chooses Modern Timeline, Storylines or Character spotlights.

**Thinking**: "I know whether I want chronology, a complete arc or a character."

**Target experience**: Compact labels distinguish the paths. No paragraph must be read before a
choice can be made.

## Stage 3: Evaluation
**Doing**: Compares Reading Lists on the selected browse screen.

**Thinking**: "How long is this? Where does it start? Is it beginner-friendly?"

**Target experience**: The dedicated screen provides search, filters, cover-led cards, scope and
preview details without crowding Home.

## Stage 4: Commitment
**Doing**: Adds a Reading List from its preview.

**Thinking**: "Did that work, and what should I read first?"

**Target experience**: The action confirms in place, the list appears in My Library, and the reading
screen names the next issue.

## Stage 5: Return
**Doing**: Reopens the app days later.

**Thinking**: "Which issue was I on?"

**Target experience**: Continue reading and saved-list progress come first. The category gateway
remains available below them when the reader wants something new.

## Emotional Arc
Curious to oriented to confident to reading.
