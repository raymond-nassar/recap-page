Real guides. Visible sources.

Reading lists are curated by people, drawn from published guides, selected by the project, or assembled from issue metadata. Each list tells you which it is.

Some publishers have explicitly permitted building from eligible public reading orders when their work is credited and linked. Others have approved credit and links without granting a broader license. Their terms and exclusions are kept separate.

Comic Book Reading Orders permits eligible public guides with credit and a link to the exact guide. Its Marvel Master Reading Order and Patreon-only orders are excluded. Comic Book Herald's recorded response approves continued crediting and linking; it is not a blanket license over its editorial work or Marvel's comics.

A few things to know.

Clear answers before you start.

Does Recap Page include comics?

No. It tracks lists and reading progress. You read comics separately with a service or edition you choose.

Does it make reading orders with AI?

No. Reading lists follow named published guides, project selections, or series metadata. Source and permission details vary by list and are linked from its card and the data sources page.

Does my progress sync automatically?

No. Each installation saves separately. Recap Page does not detect your reading or include offline comics. Export a backup or choose the supported backup to your own OneDrive or iCloud account; restoring replaces saved data.

Where does Read open?

Marvel Unlimited changes its service on December 7, 2026. When a comic has a Marvel Comics app link, Read opens it in the Marvel Comics app. For other comics, before that date Read uses the web reader when a link is recorded, with your own subscription. From that date, Read opens the general Marvel Comics website for those comics, not a link to a single comic. Marvel's FAQ
