Your Marvel reading companion

Follow a Marvel story across series, open the next unread comic, and keep your place.

Runs locally on your desktop, not a native phone app. No Recap Page account. No automatic device sync or offline comic reading. Comics and a separate Marvel Unlimited subscription are not included.

Get started Read the overview

Follow a story across series

A walkthrough with made-up progress, not someone's reading history.

  1. Choose an order. In Browse, add Civil War: An Avengers Reading List, compiled from Comic Book Herald's guide.
  2. Find your next unread issue. In this example, the first six comics are marked read. Civil War (2006) #7 is next.
  3. Choose Read. The official reader opens in a separate tab when a link is available. A missing reader link falls back to an official issue page, not guaranteed access.
  4. Return and choose Done, next yourself. New Avengers (2004) #21 becomes next; reading is not detected automatically.
  5. Stop and resume. Reopen this Reading List later, in the same browser profile, to resume at that unread comic.

Read markers are shared for the same issue across overlapping Reading Lists; each list keeps its own order.
