# Code of conduct

## The short version

Treat people with respect.
Assume good intent.
Critique the work, not the person.

## What this covers

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages, and documents.
It also covers behaviour elsewhere that targets someone
because of their involvement in this project.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Insults, demeaning comments, or hostility aimed at a person
  rather than at their work.
  This includes hostility aimed at someone for what they do not yet know.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of that kind.
- Attacks or exclusion based on someone's background, disability, identity, appearance, beliefs,
  age, or other personal characteristics.
- Publishing someone's private information,
  or anything that could identify them,
  without their clear permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions
  or repeatedly reopening settled, explained decisions
  to wear people down.

Direct criticism with reasons is welcome.
Declining a request with a clear explanation is also fine.

## Reporting a concern

Open a repository issue, sharing only what you are comfortable making public.

**If your concern is about the maintainer**,
or you are not willing to raise it publicly, use
[GitHub's own abuse reporting form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) instead.
It goes to GitHub rather than to anyone here.

There is no private maintainer channel or published contact address
for conduct concerns.
Private vulnerability reporting, enabled on 2026-08-16, is for security reports, not conduct.

## What happens then

The sole maintainer reviews the full report when available,
explains the decision, and uses the least severe response
that addresses the behaviour: a private warning, public correction,
edited or removed comment, closed thread, or blocked account.
Serious or repeated behaviour leads directly to blocking.

The maintainer cannot judge a concern about themselves.
Use GitHub's reporting route for that.

## Where this came from

Written for this project, with a structure informed by
[the Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
